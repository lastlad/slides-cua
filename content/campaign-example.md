# Nimbus Launch Campaign

## Agenda
- Where the market is
- Who we are talking to
- Channels and budget
- Timeline and next steps

## The opportunity
- Mid-market teams are priced out of enterprise observability
- 62% of surveyed teams still stitch together three separate tools
- Nobody in the category is speaking to the practitioner directly

## Campaign objectives
- Drive 4,000 trial signups in the first quarter
- Establish Nimbus as the practitioner-first choice
- Build a reusable content engine for the platform team

## Who we are talking to
- Priya, staff SRE at a 400-person fintech
- Owns on-call and the incident budget, not the purchasing decision
- Frustrated by dashboards that answer questions nobody asked
- Trusts peer recommendations and hands-on trials over analyst reports

## Channels
- Technical content and SEO as the long-term engine
- Developer podcast sponsorships for reach
- Conference presence at SRECon and KubeCon
- Lifecycle email to the existing free tier

## Measurement
- Trial signups and trial-to-paid conversion as the headline KPIs
- Organic sessions and assisted conversions for content
- Cost per qualified trial by channel

## Budget
- Content and SEO: $180k
- Events: $140k
- Paid and podcast: $95k
- Tooling and production: $35k

## Next steps
- Lock the messaging framework by the end of the month
- Brief the agency on the first content wave
- Stand up the measurement dashboard before launch
- Confirm conference slots for the second half
